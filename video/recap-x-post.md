## Long post

One week as a PayBox finalist: 10 tools to 17, and a 14x call 🚀

A week ago, Oddshot was named one of 13 finalists in the @PayBox hackathon. I didn't slow down. I kept shipping, tested it with real money, and graded my calls in public. Here's the full week.

THE IDEA

Prediction markets move before memecoins do. When the crowd on Polymarket or Kalshi suddenly bets on a story, the coins riding that story usually haven't moved yet.

Oddshot reads those odds, finds the coin, checks it's safe, and builds the swap. PayBox signs it. Oddshot never touches your keys. You just ask in ChatGPT or Claude.

The odds move first. The memes follow.

THE RUG THAT BECAME A TOOL

On Tuesday, Oddshot warned me about $NVIDIA: a pool 30 minutes old, zero organic trading. I bought $2 anyway.

Six minutes later the creator sold 478M tokens, 48% of the supply, for about $40K. The price fell 99.8%.

So I turned that rug into code. Oddshot now runs a rug autopsy on any crash and names the cause: creator dump, whale dump or pulled liquidity. It watches the creator's wallet on every coin. And it flat out refuses to build a buy on that setup.
