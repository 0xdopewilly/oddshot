How it works

When the crowd's odds on a real-world story jump (a launch, an election, a celebrity, an IPO), memecoins named after it often move in the hours after. Oddshot catches that gap in four steps, then waits for you.

Get started

No code, no keys to paste. Oddshot runs inside PayBox and talks to the AI you already use.

  1. Set up PayBoxCreate your account at paybox.sh and connect it to ChatGPT or Claude. PayBox holds your Solana wallet; your AI never sees its keys.
  2. Switch on OddshotIn PayBox, open Plugins, find Oddshot under Community plugins and enable it.
  3. Fund the walletA little SOL for network fees and some USDC to trade. Short on either? Ask your AI to "check my wallet for a $10 buy": Oddshot's top_up answers with a MoonPay link for the exact amount.
  4. Ask in plain words"What's on the Oddshot board?" is a good first question. Every trade comes back as a proposal you see before anything is signed.

Take-profits that sell themselves

Every buy comes with an exit plan sized to the coin's own volatility: two price targets and a stop. New in 1.6, the targets become Jupiter limit orders. The tokens wait in the order on-chain and sell the moment the price gets there, whether or not you're watching.

  • Sized to your buy. After a buy lands, my_orders gives the exact take-profit calls for what it brought in, so coins you already held stay out of the orders.
  • Visible and reversible. my_orders lists every open order with its target and how far away it is; cancel_order brings the tokens back.
  • The seatbelt still sees them. Tokens waiting in an order still count toward your bag, and an exit tells you which orders to cancel first.
  • Honest limits. Jupiter's orders only sell on the way up, so the stop is a price Oddshot names for you to sell at. Each order must pay out at least $5, locks about 0.0041 SOL of rent until it closes (you get it back), and pays 0.1% when it fills. Coins with a Token-2022 transfer fee can't go in an order.

The seatbelt

Every verdict cross-checks market data (DexScreener), RugCheck and Jupiter's audit, reads the token's mint for transfer fees, and follows the wallet that launched the coin.

GREENYELLOWRED

What makes a coin RED

  • Any danger flag, such as a mint authority that can print more supply.
  • Three of Oddshot's own warnings at once.
  • The rug setup: a pool under an hour old with an organic score of 0.
  • The creator selling 1% of supply (or $500) in the last hour, holding 30% or more, or hiding a launch bag in other wallets.

What the seatbelt does about it

  • ape refuses to buy a RED coin, and never blocks a sell: you can always get out.
  • Slippage is capped at 10% and price impact at 5% on buys (15% on sells).
  • seatbelt calls EXIT on a rug flag or a dying story, TRIM on a cooling one or a risk-off macro, HOLD otherwise.
  • When a check can't be read, the report says so instead of calling the coin clean.

Odds from both crowds

Two prediction markets, read side by side, plus the tools that keep Oddshot honest about them.

Polymarket + Kalshi

A story whose odds move 3 points or more on both venues in a day is marked BOTH MARKETS and ranks higher. If Kalshi is down, the board answers from Polymarket.

Sector focus

Follow one sector on the board, the radar and the movers: stocks, ai, politics, sports, gaming, crypto, celebrities, or any keyword.

Macro board

Fed, recession, crypto-law, Bitcoin-reserve, tariff and shutdown odds, scored risk-on or risk-off, with a call on SOL, BTC and ETH. Most days it says NO PUSH.

Lag check

The idea is that odds move first and memes follow. lag_check measures it story by story, and says NO REACTION when that's what happened.

Receipts

Any past call graded from real price and odds history: HIT, MISS or FLAT. Misses are reported like hits.

Noise filtered

Sports, weather, price ladders, word-mention markets and seat-range brackets never reach the board.

Your wallet, your call

No keys, ever

Oddshot never holds or sees a key. It builds unsigned transactions; your PayBox wallet signs them.

You set the rules

PayBox's access settings decide whether you approve every trade yourself or let your agent act within the limits you choose.

Nothing stored

Each request is answered from live public data (prediction markets, DEXs, the Solana chain) and forgotten.

Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely risky. Oddshot reports signals and safety checks; a GREEN verdict is not a guarantee, and one good call is not a track record.

What does it cost?

Oddshot itself is free. You pay Solana network fees and the swap route's costs, 0.1% when a take-profit order fills, and MoonPay's fees if you top up through it (MoonPay's minimum is $20).

Which chain and which coins?

Solana: memecoins, plus SOL, BTC (Coinbase's cbBTC) and ETH (Wormhole Ether) against USDC. The majors are pinned by address, so lookalike tokens never pass as the real thing.

Why was my trade refused?

Usually the coin was RED, the pool was too thin for the size, or the wallet was short on SOL or USDC. Ask your AI to run check_token or top_up; for take-profit orders, my_orders says exactly why.

Can it set a stop-loss?

Not as an automatic order. Jupiter's orders that work inside PayBox only sell on the way up. Oddshot names the stop price from your exit plan, and the seatbelt flags rugs, creator dumps and dying stories. Selling at the stop is up to you.

Does it trade tokenized stocks?

No. The stocks focus follows stock-themed memecoins and company markets. Tokenized shares are securities, and Oddshot never suggests them; if you hold some, the seatbelt treats them as issuer coins, not rugs.
