The rug that became a tool

On Oct 27, Oddshot flagged $NVIDIA as LATE, with zero organic trading on a pool about 30 minutes old. A $2 buy went through anyway at 10:05 UTC (Solscan).

Six minutes later the creator sold 478M tokens, 48% of the supply, for about $40K, and the price fell 99.8%.

NOWrug_autopsy reads that crash as CREATOR DUMP, the same setup is RED, and Oddshot refuses to build the buy.

Safety and limits

Oddshot proposes and PayBox signs: the plugin never holds a key, and every trade is a transaction the user's PayBox wallet approves.

Safety

  • Checks on every coin: liquidity, holder concentration, mint and freeze authority, organic trading, and the creator's own wallet. RED means Oddshot won't build the buy.
  • Seatbelt: for each coin held, hold, trim or exit, with the exact sell ready to approve.
  • Tokenized stocks stay out: discovery never surfaces tokenized securities.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Take-profit only, no stop-loss. Jupiter's keyless orders can only wait for a rise. The seatbelt watches for rugs, creator dumps and dying stories, not the price.
  • Jupiter's terms apply: at least $5 per order, about 0.0041 SOL of rent per order (returned when it closes), 0.1% fee on fill.
  • ChatGPT sometimes blocks trade calls with its own safety check before Oddshot runs. Trades work reliably in Claude.
  • About 5 seconds per call, PayBox's budget for a plugin. When a source is slow, Oddshot says so instead of hanging.
